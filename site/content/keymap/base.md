Layer 0, always on: QWERTY with home-row mods, and the thumbs doing the rest.

{{< layer "BASE" >}}

## What's on it

- **Home-row mods**: <kbd>S</kbd> Alt, <kbd>D</kbd> Ctrl, <kbd>F</kbd> Shift
  on the left; <kbd>J</kbd> Shift, <kbd>K</kbd> Ctrl, <kbd>L</kbd> Alt on the
  right. Same finger, same modifier on both hands. Tap for the letter, hold
  for the modifier; the rules that keep fast typing from firing them are on
  [Home-row mods & timing](../hold-tap/).
- **Left thumb**: `mo(SYS|NUM)` · `F11` · `GUI` · `mo(NAV)` · `Backspace`.
- **Right thumb**: `Space` · `mo(CODE)` · `Caps Word` · `F12`, or AltGr when
  held · `mo(MEDIA)`.
- **Joystick**: volume up and down, play/pause, next track, and mute on the
  centre press: media without holding a layer. Position 52 is a second Mute
  key.
- **Encoder**: volume. Editable live in [Studio](../../docs/studio/).
- **Delete** is `mo(CODE)` + `Backspace`: hold CODE with the right thumb, tap
  Backspace with the left. Two hands, no hold-tap.

AltGr sits on the thumb rather than on a letter key, so accented letters
(AltGr+E, AltGr+O) are reachable with either hand, and fast typing doesn't
turn a letter into AltGr.

## Getting to the other layers

Hold `mo(NAV)` or `mo(SYS|NUM)` with the left thumb, `mo(CODE)` or
`mo(MEDIA)` with the right. The game layers start from SYS|NUM; see
[Gaming layers](../gaming/).
