## The board

The **Eyelash Sofle** is a wireless split keyboard:

- two halves, each on a **nice!nano v2** (nRF52840) with a **nice!view**
  display;
- a five-way **joystick** and a rotary **encoder** (left half);
- RGB **underglow**, seven LEDs a half, one per column plus the thumb.

[Getting started](../getting-started/) covers how the firmware uses them.
