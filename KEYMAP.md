# Keymap

The keymap reference lives on the site, generated from
[`config/eyelash_sofle.keymap`](config/eyelash_sofle.keymap) on every build so it
can't drift from the firmware:

- **[Overview](https://sofleii.dlu.one/keymap/):** the full render and how to
  reach each layer
- **Layers:** [BASE](https://sofleii.dlu.one/keymap/base/) ·
  [NAV](https://sofleii.dlu.one/keymap/nav/) ·
  [CODE](https://sofleii.dlu.one/keymap/code/) ·
  [MEDIA](https://sofleii.dlu.one/keymap/media/) ·
  [SYS|NUM](https://sofleii.dlu.one/keymap/sys-num/) ·
  [Gaming layers](https://sofleii.dlu.one/keymap/gaming/)
- **[Home-row mods & timing](https://sofleii.dlu.one/keymap/hold-tap/):** the
  hold-tap behaviors, their timing, the index-finger exception and tuning tips
- **[Combos](https://sofleii.dlu.one/keymap/combos/)**
- **[Underglow](https://sofleii.dlu.one/docs/underglow/):** per-layer colours,
  base moods and brightness
